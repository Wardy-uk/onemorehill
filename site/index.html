One More Hill

While you're there, bag them all

There are 6,081 standing Ordnance Survey trig pillars in Britain. 1,510 of them sit within 10 metres of a named hill — a Munro, a Wainwright, an Ethel, a Marilyn. Climb one and you've bagged both.

Nobody had published that cross-reference, because the two databases have never been joined: the hill database holds no trig points, and the trig database holds no hill lists. So we joined them. Here it is, free.

A thing we noticed. The Full House — every Munro, Munro Top, Corbett, Fiona, Donald and Furth — is officially 1,072 summits. But only 1,042 of them are distinct hills: 30 sit in two lists at once. Ben Cleuch is a Fiona and a Donald. Merrick is a Corbett and a Donald. Thirty of those walks tick two boxes, and nothing tells you which.
